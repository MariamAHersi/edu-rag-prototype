           Assessment Requirements                                                
                                                                                  
          This is a group work (up to 2 students per group). Each of team member may
          receive same mark for their submission.                                 
                                                                                  
          Please clearly indicate all students’ name and IDs in your submission.  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Task:                                                                   
                                                                                  
          In this section, you are expected to describe an overview of your idea of the DBMS you
          want to design and develop, what are the business questions based on what you will
          cover in your system. You will need a good logical relational database management
                                                                                  
          system representation (ERD) based on your proposed idea. This includes Formal Data
          Modelling with detailed features of your system.                        
                                                                                  
          Your submission must be a single report (PDF) and should contain:       
                                                                                  
            •  Business system summary based on the chosen topic.                 
                 •  Describe your business idea in detail.                        
                 •  Describe work objectives.                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
            •  An ERD (logical schema) of your proposed idea using an appropriate tool (It is
               required that you ERD contains 12-14 distinguish Entity sets in it).
                 •  Indicate key attribute for each entity set.                   
                 •  Indicate cardinality for each relationship.                   
                                                                                  
                 •  Indicate participation for each entity related to each relationship.
                                                                                  
            •  A diagram (physical data model) which fully match the ERD, should clearly
               shows:                                                             
                                                                                  
                 •  All tables                                                    
                 •  Columns within each table                                     
                 •  Constraints including data types and Keys (Primary Key and Foreign Key)
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Deliverables (what you will need to submit): a single report (PDF)      
                                                                                  
          Additional Information:                                                 
                                                                                  
          To properly design your business database, you may need to perform some literature
          review. If you have used ideas from anywhere other than the lecture notes and tutorial
          examples (e.g., from a book, the Internet, or a fellow student) then include a reference
                                                                                  
          showing where ideas came from and label your content carefully to show which parts are
          borrowed.                                                               
                                                                                  
          Please be aware that diagrams in your submission must be fully original.

| Assessment Success Guidance To achieve maximum success in this task: • Select a suitable business idea that clearly requires a relational database (complex data, multiple entities, clear relationships). • Define clear objectives and key business questions your system will address. • Develop a detailed and accurate ERD with 12–14 entities, showing primary keys, relationships, cardinality, and participation. • Create a consistent physical data model that fully matches your ERD, including tables, data types, and key constraints. • Present a professional, well-structured report (single PDF) with clear sections and original, well-labelled diagrams. • Demonstrate professionalism through clear communication, correct referencing, and attention to detail. |

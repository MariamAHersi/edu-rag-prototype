| Assessment Requirements Multiple choice questions: this is a 12 question test available on Moodle under “Minibrief 2”. The test may be completed open book (i.e. the use of notes and the internet is permitted and encouraged) and is worth a maximum of 60 marks. The test may only be attempted once – as such, it is strongly recommended that students complete it in one sitting. Creative coding section: create an interactive fish tank animation. The animation must feature a fish that follows the mouse cursor around the screen and blows bubbles. Additional marks are available for decoration, both of the fish itself and of the environment, as well as for code readability. This is worth a maximum of 40 marks. Recommended steps: 1. Create a fish out of basic shapes such as triangles, ellipses and circles. 2. Make the fish follow the mouse cursor, perhaps by using translate() or co-ordinate addition. 3. Using arrays, make bubbles that follow the mouse cursor, drifting upwards after each frame. 4. Add a “wobble” to the circles using random(). 5. Using map() and fill(), make the bubbles gradually fade away. |

          2025 AI Student Guide                                                   
          What AI Can and Can’t Do                                                
                                                                                  
            •  AI can become a useful learning assistant and help you:            
            •  Summarise articles and concepts.                                   
            •  Brainstorm ideas.                                                  
                                                                                  
            •  Improve your writing (grammar, spelling, structure).               
            •  Find sources for initial searches (always verify them!).           
                                                                                  
            •  Quiz you on your knowledge.                                        
            •  Help you develop a studying schedule.                              
                                                                                  
            •  Organise and summarise your notes.                                 
            •  Develop your critical thinking skills, through interactive dialogue.
            •  Develop your inquiring skills.                                     
                                                                                  
          AI should not be used to:                                               
            •  Create entire assessments for you. Always check the assessment guidance about
               what is permitted and what is not. AI should not simply do the work for you.
                                                                                  
            •  Take exams or quizzes on your behalf.                              
            •  Generate ideas or arguments that you don’t understand (although AI can explain
               ideas and arguments to you).                                       
                                                                                  
            •  Fake citations or research.                                        
          Stay Honest and Transparent                                             
            •  Acknowledge all use of AI, just as you would for other help; Cite AI generated
               content.                                                           
                                                                                  
            •  Don’t submit AI-generated work as your own—it’s considered academic
               misconduct.                                                        
            •  Remember: AI can make mistakes! Always fact-check before using its output.
                                                                                  
            •  Remember: AI can be biased and reproduce inappropriate or excluding social
               stereotypes.                                                       
            •  Make sure you read critically to verify the AI outputs. You are responsible for the
                                                                                  
               outputs generated by AI tools.                                     
            •  You need to have a good understanding and knowledge of the subject to critically
               evaluate AI outputs and prompt further.                            
          Use AI to Learn, Not as a Shortcut                                      
                                                                                  
            •  AI is a tool, not a replacement for your own thinking. Use it to:  
            •  Get help structuring your thoughts.                                
                                                                                  
            •  Check your understanding of a topic.                               
            •  Improve your writing, creative process or knowledge development while keeping
               your unique voice and using your own skills.
